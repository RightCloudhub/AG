# Helix Compute: Headquarters and Market Position

Helix Compute is headquartered in Munich. Helix Compute was founded in 2011 and sells enterprise servers across Europe and Asia.

Helix Compute produces HelixCore Server and the HelixEdge Appliance, a compact edge-computing box released in 2024.

Helix Compute is a strategic partner of Meridian Capital following the Helix Series C 2023 financing round.

Helix Compute and NovaTech Industries remain direct competitors in the enterprise server market.
