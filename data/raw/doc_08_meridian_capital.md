# Meridian Capital Firm Overview

Meridian Capital is a private investment firm headquartered in London. It focuses on growth-stage investments in enterprise hardware and infrastructure software.

The CEO of Meridian Capital is Sofia Lindqvist. Sofia Lindqvist previously worked at Orion Systems as Chief Financial Officer.

Meridian Capital led the Helix Series C 2023 financing round, a funding event valued at USD 320 million. Helix Compute participated in Helix Series C 2023 as the issuing company.

Two notable executives in this corpus are Meridian Capital alumni: Elena Varga, now CEO of Apex Holdings, and Priya Nair, now CEO of Helix Compute.
