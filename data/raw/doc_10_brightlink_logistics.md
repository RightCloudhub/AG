# BrightLink Logistics Profile

BrightLink Logistics is a logistics company headquartered in Rotterdam and a subsidiary of Apex Holdings.

The CEO of BrightLink Logistics is Amara Osei. Amara Osei previously worked at Harbor Components as Director of Operations.

BrightLink Logistics provides shipping services to NovaTech Industries and Helix Compute distribution centers. BrightLink Logistics also supplies logistics services to SiliconForge.

BrightLink Logistics is a partner of Harbor Components for cross-border component freight between Busan and Rotterdam.
