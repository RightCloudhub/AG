# Orion Systems Company Profile

Orion Systems is a cloud software company headquartered in Seattle. Orion Systems was founded in 2004 by Daniel Okafor, who still serves as its chairman.

The current CEO of Orion Systems is Grace Liu. Grace Liu previously worked at Cascade Analytics as Head of Platform Engineering before joining Orion Systems in 2021.

Orion Systems produces the CloudNexus Platform, a hybrid-cloud management suite. CloudNexus Platform is certified to run on QuantumEdge Server hardware.

Orion Systems is a strategic partner of NovaTech Industries. The two companies jointly sell bundled CloudNexus Platform and QuantumEdge Server configurations to enterprise customers.

Elena Varga and Marcus Chen are both Orion Systems alumni; they worked together on cloud product strategy during the 2010s.
