# SiliconForge Semiconductor Profile

SiliconForge is a semiconductor company headquartered in Hsinchu. SiliconForge designs high-performance server processors.

The CEO of SiliconForge is Kenji Watanabe. Kenji Watanabe founded SiliconForge in 2009 after leaving a career in chip design.

SiliconForge produces the SF-9 Processor, which powers both QuantumEdge Server and HelixCore Server.

In 2025, SiliconForge acquired Lumen Photonics, an optical interconnect startup. The deal is recorded as the Lumen Acquisition 2025 event, in which SiliconForge participated as the acquirer.

Lumen Photonics supplies optical modules for HelixCore Server. Lumen Photonics is headquartered in Hsinchu.
